## Rollback and stop conditions

- The new store adds nullable `observation_json` transactionally and sets `user_version=2`; historical rows keep null observations. Old binaries cannot read that schema. Switching the unit back alone is not rollback.
- On unknown schema, failed backup, unsafe path/ownership, unexpected profile/source, provider denial, ambiguous transfer, failed artifact retention, or damaged evidence: stop rollout for that service. Preserve evidence and contain on the current revision with `live_enabled=false` when safe. Do not weaken validation or enable fallback to obtain a green result.
- For binary rollback, stop service and publication writer, preserve upgraded state/publication/artifact roots in exact inactive locations, and restore the pre-upgrade snapshot with its separately retained manifest digest into the **same original absent paths** using a default-off config. Retain post-upgrade state for reconciliation; do not merge or replay provider jobs. Only then select the old compatible unit/config and verify default-off readiness and retained records. Failure leaves partial restore roots inactive.
- Backup excludes credentials, raw quarantine input, scratch, source and external deployment targets. Restore consumes the shared 4 GiB I/O budget twice for payload verification/copy, so a complete snapshot may still exceed restore capacity; establish that the payload fits (at most 2 GiB before other bounds), 4,096 files, 512 MiB/file and 180-second deadline. An incomplete manifest is not a backup. No public-site restore or PostgreSQL operation is part of this task.
